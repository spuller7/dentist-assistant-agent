# Insurance, Prep, and Office Policies

Riverside Family Dental accepts most PPO plans, including Delta Dental, Cigna, Aetna, and MetLife. We do not accept Medicaid in this demo office.

Bring a photo ID and your insurance card to the first visit. If you have X-rays from another office from the last 12 months, bring them or ask that office to send them.

Routine cleanings are usually 45–60 minutes. Pediatric visits are 30–45 minutes. Oral surgery consults are 30 minutes; extractions are scheduled separately after the consult.

The office is not a medical ER. Chest pain, trouble breathing, or facial trauma with loss of consciousness should go to the emergency room.

HIPAA: staff only discuss a patient's visit with that patient or a listed guardian.
