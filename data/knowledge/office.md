# Riverside Family Dental — Hours and Location

Riverside Family Dental is open:

- Monday through Friday: 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM
- Saturday: 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM (hygiene and short visits only)
- Sunday: closed

The last weekday appointment starts at 4:00 PM. Weekday visits are hourly from 9:00 AM through 4:00 PM, including 10:00 AM and noon. Saturday appointments are 9:00, 10:00, 11:00, or 12:00.

The office phone is 555-0100. For swelling, uncontrolled bleeding, or a knocked-out tooth after hours, call that number and follow the emergency prompt.

We are at 1402 Riverside Avenue. Street parking is available on the west side of the building. Please arrive 10 minutes early for regular visits and 20 minutes early if you are a new patient.

Cancellations need 24 hours notice. Late cancels may be charged a $40 fee.

Appointments may be made with a prefferred dentist.
